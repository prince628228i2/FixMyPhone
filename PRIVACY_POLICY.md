# Fix My Phone — Privacy Policy Draft

Fix My Phone is designed to process information only as needed to perform a user-requested task.

**Accessibility data:** When the Accessibility Service is enabled, the app may read the active UI hierarchy (such as visible text, labels, bounds and interaction properties) to carry out a requested automation. The app does not intentionally persist screen snapshots.

**Voice:** Speech is captured only when voice input is started. Depending on the Android speech-recognition implementation, audio/speech may be processed by the selected recognition provider.

**AI providers:** If you configure Gemini or OpenAI, the task prompt and the minimum screen context needed for planning may be sent to that provider. Do not configure a provider if you do not want task data sent to it.

**API keys:** Provider keys are stored locally using Android encrypted preferences.

**Contacts and phone:** If you grant permissions, the app can resolve contacts and initiate a call when explicitly requested. Calling requires an explicit confirmation in the app.

**Sharing:** The app does not sell personal information. Third-party AI and speech providers may process information according to their own policies.
